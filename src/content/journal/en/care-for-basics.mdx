## Read the care label first

Different fabrics need different care. Follow the instructions attached to the garment you own. The products in this store are fictional, so their photographs are not a substitute for a real care label.

## Make time to sort

Separate items that may transfer colour and check pockets before washing. Turn printed pieces inside out if their care label recommends it. Avoid guessing a temperature from the garment's appearance alone.

## Store for the next wear

Let clothes dry fully before putting them away. Folding soft knits can help keep a drawer tidy. Choose a routine that fits your space and the instructions for each piece.
