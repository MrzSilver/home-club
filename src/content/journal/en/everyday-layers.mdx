## Start with what feels comfortable

A tee is an easy starting point. Add a hoodie when the air cools, leaving enough room at the shoulders to move freely. Try each piece together before deciding on a size.

## Keep the colours connected

Choose one neutral colour as your base. A darker outer layer adds definition without needing a perfect match. Sand, grey and black can be starting points; the combination is yours.

## Adjust through the day

Wear the outer layer open or carry it when you warm up. This is a styling idea for a demo wardrobe, rather than technical advice about protection from weather.
